## What v11 changes (on top of v10)
1. **GLUTH (8-turn glut window).**
   - RACEGATE skips the sale reservation for any product quoted at or below base. In glutted books we therefore sold only at the tape's own turn.
   - Many ladder mirrors sell those lots 1–3 turns earlier (e.g. Hangyu Yu, rhythm master), or drip them in small lots (Agricola, mikelou1, Roxy, Ziad).
   - GLUTH reserves glutted lots only when the tape sells them within **8 turns**. Everything else in RACEGATE is unchanged.
   - It is implemented as a layer that re-binds `_RACE_ORIG_RESERVE`, so the public `main.py` stays a byte-exact prefix. It was verified identical to the in-file patch.
2. **SLOT.**
   - Same-turn sells settle slot by slot in lockstep.
   - Against a mirror's (parent) order, SLOT picks the permutation of our premium sells that maximizes won races minus lost races, where the value of a race is the revenue gained by selling ahead of an equal rival lot.
   - It triggers about 2 turns per game.
3. **Buy-first.**
   - Wheat and fertilizer buys go ahead of fixed-price orders (seeds, animals, hires, land) and of small fertilizer sells. Mirrors that list their wheat buy first were winning 85 buy races to our 9 across 61 v9 games.
   - Only applied with ample cash (≥ 2× cost + 1000).
