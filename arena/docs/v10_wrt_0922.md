# Kaggriculture: v10, same-step wheat round trips (22 Sep)

**Status (22 Sep).** v9 **2811.2**, v8 2804.6. **v10 submitted** ("v10", plain `main.py`, 1,041,309 bytes, sha256 `85014f41…`). The active pair becomes **v9 + v10**, and v8 drops out of the latest-two window.

## The leak: our own wheat trades are public
- Three route tapes buy wheat at step t, right before a town draw, and sell the same amount at t+1 (sometimes t+2). They are routes **103, 112 and 124**, plus five t+2 trips in 103/104/112.
  - Shop pairs: Bakery/Brunch, Brunch/Brunch, PetCafe/Bakery, FarmersMkt/Brunch, Pizza/Smoothie; route 104 is Bakery/FM and FM/FM.
  - About 7% of seeds; route 103 has 39 trips of 48–90 units.
- **A. R. SEKKAT runs a copy that does the trip inside one step**: BUY q in slot 0, SELL q in slot 1.
  - Orders settle slot by slot in lockstep, so its buy moves in step with ours and its sell lands at the top of both buys.
  - We then sell the next step into a lower book.
  - Transfer is about q²/2 × slope per trip.
- In the recorded loss, wheat volumes were identical (3,686 vs 3,683 sold), but SEKKAT netted **$5.4k more on wheat**. We lost the game by $4.7k.
- The public omw lineage (dm29, mv13, v53, and plain copies on the ladder) all run the split version of the trip.

## The fix (WRT layer, `layers/wrt.py`, appended last)
- When the tape's buy at t is matched by a same-size tape sale at t+1 or t+2, we buy in slot 0 and sell in slot 1 of step t.
- The parent's later wheat sales are then stripped up to the lot size, including any reservation pulling that sale forward. There is no rebuy.
- Our stock path is unchanged from v9; only the timing of the trade moves.
- Against a SEKKAT-style copy this ties. Against a plain copy we take the edge.
- A same-step trip nets exactly zero against an unchanged book, because buy quotes are taken at inventory−1.

## Why mirrors beat us in the other close losses
- Most same-base mirrors sell glutted milk, wool and strawberry 1–2 steps before the tape's own sale; examples are Hangyu Yu and rhythm master.
- Our RACEPX/RACEGATE gates hold those lots until the tape sale. Wool alone is −$10.3k of price effect over 40 v9 games.
- Removing the gates doesn't pay on balance (table above), so this is a rock–paper–scissors race, not a free fix.
