## A cleaner test method
- **Prefix replay** (`pfx.py`, `xcross.py`): replay a recorded game move for move up to step S (360 = day 15), then let a candidate play our seat against the opponent's recorded moves.
- **Decoupled randomness after S** (`decouple.py`): Kaggle draws weeds for farm 0, farm 1 and the next shop from one random stream, consuming one draw per empty tile. So any change in how many tiles we leave empty re-draws the opponent's weeds and every future shop. The patch gives each farm and the shop draw their own stream from day S on. A candidate and its baseline then face the same town.
- **Why the old counterfactuals were unreliable:** v9 in an elite seat from step 0 already differs on day 0–1. The open-loop opponent then fails purchases (one game: opponent $131k recorded → $60k counterfactual).
- **Closed-loop panels** (`pairs.py`, `panel.py --decouple 0`) against public agents, same seeds, both seats.
